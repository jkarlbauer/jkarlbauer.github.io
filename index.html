Julian Karlbauer

AI Engineer & Applied Researcher. I work at the intersection of generative AI and spatial computing — building systems that turn machine learning into things people can actually use. That means multimodal GenAI pipelines, computer vision on edge devices, 3D generation, and LLM-powered tools, often in industrial or XR contexts. I've shipped prototypes and production systems at Siemens, Audi, and a Silicon Valley startup, and published research at leading ACM Conferences.
